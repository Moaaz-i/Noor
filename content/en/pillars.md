The pillars are practical: what the heart and tongue say in the two testifications, and what the body and wealth do in prayer, zakat, fasting and the pilgrimage. This is the agreed classification, reported from the Prophet ﷺ when he was asked about Islam.

:::Ayah text="فَأَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتُوا الزَّكَاةَ وَاعْتَصِمُوا بِاللَّهِ هُوَ مَوْلَاكُمْ" ref="الحج — ٧٨" tr="Establish prayer, give zakat, and hold fast to Allah — He is your Master." note="The verse gathers the two pillars of action in one context."
:::

:::Section title="The pillars one by one"
:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="①" title="The two testifications" kicker="First pillar"
“There is no god but God and Muhammad is His messenger.” With it one enters Islam, and monotheism becomes word and deed.
:::
:::Card icon="②" title="Prayer" kicker="Second pillar"
Five daily prayers at fixed times. The first thing a servant is judged on, and the thing that prevents immorality.
:::
:::Card icon="③" title="Zakat" kicker="Third pillar"
A fixed share of accumulated wealth given yearly to the poor — it purifies money and repairs the social order.
:::
:::Card icon="④" title="Fasting" kicker="Fourth pillar"
Abstaining from food and drink from dawn to sunset in Ramadan: training of the will, simplicity and solidarity.
:::
:::Card icon="⑤" title="Hajj" kicker="Fifth pillar"
The pilgrimage to the House of God in its seasons, for those able to make the journey — one gathering, one call.
:::
:::

:::Section title='Key takeaways'
:::Cards cols='3'
:::Card icon='1' title='Duty fits your capacity'
“Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity” — Al-Baqarah 286: what is beyond you is not asked of you, and what is within reach is not postponed.
:::
:::Card icon='2' title='Intention changes the weight'
A pillar done with a clear intention is a different thing from a routine done on autopilot — name, before you act, that you are doing it for God alone.
:::
:::Card icon='3' title='Small and steady builds it'
Ten minutes every day outlasts a single hour you carry into day one and forget by day three.
:::
:::

:::Section title="Quick questions"
:::FAQ
:::QA q="Are there only five pillars?"
Yes — that is the classification in the hadith above. The six pillars of faith (God, His angels, books, messengers, the Last Day and decree) belong to belief rather than practice.
:::
:::QA q="What if I cannot perform the Hajj?"
Hajj is binding only on the able: “And proclaim to the people the Hajj; they will come to you on foot…” Whoever truly cannot is excused.
:::
:::QA q="How do the pillars relate to belief?"
The pillars are the deed that confirms the creed: oneness of God in word is the testification, in the heart is awe of Him, and in action are the pillars.
:::
:::QA q="Where do I start if I am new to Islam?"
Begin with the two testifications, then prayer: learn its ablution and its times, and keep it at its appointed hour. Do not carry all five pillars in one week — steady ground beats a burst that burns out in days.
:::
