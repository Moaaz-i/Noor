Belief is the heart of the message: affirming God alone as Creator and Sustainer, and what was reported from Him of news and guidance. In Islam tawhid is three: of lordship, of worship, and of God’s names and attributes.

:::Section title="The six pillars of faith"
:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="☀" title="Faith in God"
One Creator, without partner; in His hand is dominion, He is not unjust to the weight of an atom, and none may question Him about what He does.
:::
:::Card icon="✨" title="Faith in angels"
Created beings who do not disobey God; they record deeds and manage affairs by His permission.
:::
:::Card icon="📜" title="Faith in the scriptures"
The scrolls, the Psalms, the Gospel, the Torah — and the Qur’an as the final, preserved scripture.
:::
:::Card icon="🕊️" title="Faith in the messengers"
Adam, Abraham, Moses, Jesus and Muhammad ﷺ; the last of them Muhammad, and all of them one community.
:::
:::Card icon="⏳" title="Faith in the Last Day"
Resurrection after death, judgement, then Paradise or Hell — the root of balance and accountability.
:::
:::Card icon="⚖️" title="Faith in divine decree"
Good and bad from God; a belief that neither cancels responsibility nor excuses effort.
:::
:::

:::Section title="Three balances"
:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="📏" title="Oneness & action"
Word without deed is sterile, deed without belief is blind; Islam joins them in one system.
:::
:::Card icon="🤲" title="Hope & awe"
Hope in God’s mercy prevents despair; awe of His punishment prevents carelessness.
:::
:::Card icon="🧍" title="Decree & effort"
Knowledge of decree does not cancel choice: make us of the grateful, not of the indolent.
:::

:::Section title="Principles of the Shariah"
| Principle | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Justice | Putting everything in its place | No oppression, no vendetta |
| Mercy | Tenderness toward those in need | Social cohesion, protection of the weak |
| Trustworthiness | Guarding what you are entrusted with | Confidence and sound transactions |
| Freedom | “No compulsion in religion” | Invitation by wisdom, not by force |
| Accountability | Everyone answers for what they begin | Self-correction before courts |

:::

:::Section title='Key takeaways'
:::Cards cols='3'
:::Card icon='1' title='Faith shows in its fruit'
A word without a deed is a claim; belief is seen in your patience, your fairness and your trustworthiness long before it is seen in an argument.
:::
:::Card icon='2' title='Oneness lifts your hope'
Whoever turns both desire and fear to God is not humbled before anyone beneath Him, and does not despair of His mercy at the first stumble.
:::
:::Card icon='3' title='The six roots hold together'
Neglect one root and whatever rests on it tilts: faith in decree keeps you from panic, and faith in the Last Day keeps you from grasping at this world alone.
:::

:::Section title='Live it this week'
:::Timeline
:::Event when='Every night' title='Five minutes of reflection'
Before sleep, read a single verse, think about it for two minutes, and write one line in a notebook about what you understood from it.
:::
:::Event when='Once a week' title='One question, answered'
Pick the belief question that has been bothering you, put it on paper, wait a week, then answer it from a trusted book of tafsir or creed.
:::
:::Event when='End of month' title='A short audit'
Sit for ten minutes and ask: which of the six roots is weakest in me this month? Write down one small step to strengthen it next month.

:::Section title='Quick questions'
:::FAQ
:::QA q='Can I believe before I know every detail?'
Yes. The six roots are stated in summary, and that is enough to affirm them; the detail comes in time, through learning and asking. You do not have to be a scholar before you can believe.
:::
:::QA q='What do I do if I start doubting a root of faith?'
Do not leave a doubt silent: write it down, spend a week with a trustworthy source on it, and ask someone whose knowledge you trust. A question that gets an answer beats a silence that just hardens.
:::
:::QA q='Does oneness mean something different in every age?'
No. Oneness is one root no age can cancel: the laws given to earlier communities changed, but sincerity to God alone is what He has asked of people in every time.
:::
