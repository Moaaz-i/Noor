The Qur’an made marriage a covenant between husband and wife, and made the home the first school where a child learns patience, fairness and mercy. What begins with a sound choice ends in a home whose neighbours and members alike speak well of it. What follows is a short guide to the stages of family life and the rights within it.

:::Hadith text='خَيْرُكُمْ خَيْرُكُمْ لِأَهْلِهِ وَأَنَا خَيْرُكُمْ لِأَهْلِي' ref='Sunan al-Tirmidhi' tr='“The best of you are the best to their families, and I am the best of you to my family.”' note='Rank in faith is measured at home: whoever is gracious in the mosque should be gracious with his family.'
:::

:::Section title='From choosing to inheritance'
:::Cards cols='3'
:::Card icon='👫' title='Choosing a spouse' kicker='Religion first'
Religion before wealth and looks: wealth runs out, looks fade, and it is religion together with character that keeps a home upright. Take counsel from someone whose own faith you trust, ask God for what is best, and leave aside whatever has no value beside religion.
:::
:::Card icon='📜' title='The marriage contract' kicker='Offer and acceptance'
A simple contract: clear words of offer and acceptance, honourable witnesses, a known dower, and the consent of both sides. It is the lightest of contracts and the deepest in effect, and any sound condition they agree to is honoured unless it contradicts the sacred law.
:::
:::Card icon='🤝' title='Mutual rights' kicker='Affection and mercy'
“And of His signs is that He created for you mates from among yourselves, that you may dwell in tranquillity with them, and He placed between you affection and mercy.” Upkeep and housing with fairness, kindness in return, and good character read in every detail of the house.
:::
:::Card icon='🧒' title='Children' kicker='Blessing and duty'
“The wealth and children are the adornment of the life of this world.” Their right is the best of names, honour, upbringing upon oneness of God rather than indulgence — and a daughter is equal to a son in dignity and in duty.
:::
:::Card icon='👴' title='Honouring parents' kicker='Right after oneness'
“And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and to parents, good treatment” — placed straight after monotheism. Kindness to them in old age is the widest door of provision and life.
:::
:::Card icon='📊' title='Inheritance' kicker='Rights, not preferences'
The estate is divided by its causes: fixed shares named in the Book, residuary shares for those the fixed shares leave out, a bequest within at most a third, payment of debts before the division, and no share for a killer.
:::
:::
:::

:::Section title='The stages of building a family'
A family does not begin with a marriage contract but with the upbringing that comes years before it. Each stage has its own mark, and what is neglected at one stage weighs on the ones after it.

:::Ayah text='وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِّتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً' ref='الروم — ٢١' tr='Tranquillity, affection and mercy — three aims joined in a single mate.' note='وهذه الثلاثة هي الميزان الذي تُقاس به رحلة الأسرة.'
:::

:::Timeline
:::Event when='Childhood' title='Upbringing'
From birth to seven: winning the heart through affection, play and safety, and teaching the pillars of Islam with gentleness rather than blame.
:::
:::Event when='Age seven' title='The age of duty'
Responsibility begins: prayer is taught and then kept, and body and mind are trained to guard their time.
:::
:::Event when='Adolescence' title='Watchfulness'
An attention not to be wasted: education through dialogue, directing the instincts toward what is lawful, and good company before advice.
:::
:::Event when='Adulthood' title='Marriage'
A choice made on faith and character, a contract with witnesses and consent, and the start of a covenant between two people equal in right.
:::
:::Event when='The home' title='Founding the home'
A lawful livelihood, and a home measured by fairness, compatibility, and the remembrance of God at its beginning and its end.
:::

:::Section title='Quick questions'
:::FAQ
:::QA q='What does a sound choice look like?'
Three things: sincere religion, good character and a balanced mind — together with compatibility in upbringing and surroundings. Take counsel from someone whose faith you trust, ask God for what is best, and pass over whatever changes with time when it opposes religion.
:::
:::QA q='Is anyone ever forced into a marriage?'
There is no compulsion in religion and none in choosing a spouse. The virgin is not married until her permission is taken — “her silence is her consent” (reported by al-Bukhari). Someone who cannot manage alone may be helped by a guardian, but no one is bound by a contract against their will.
:::
:::QA q='What right do children have in their upbringing?'
A right they hold before you do: kindness while they are small, fairness between them, good names, and example rather than commands — let them see oneness of God in your home before they hear a sermon about it. Correction comes with gentleness before firmness, and never with harshness.
:::
