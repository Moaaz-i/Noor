If you have never read about Islam before, this page is written for you alone:
plain language, no jargon, and an order that carries you from zero to a clear
understanding. The first rule is that you need to know nothing before you
begin — you begin, and then you understand.

The word **Islam** means surrender and submission: surrendering the heart to
God alone, and following what He sent. The whole message compresses into one
word: **tawhid** (monotheism) — nothing deserves worship in truth but God.

:::Ayah text="إِنَّ الدِّينَ عِندَ اللَّهِ الْإِسْلَامُ" ref="Ale Imran — 3:19" tr="The religion God accepts is called Islam: a surrender to Him in monotheism and right action."
:::

:::Section title="Where does a person start?"
Three things in sequence — none of them can stand alone:

:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="1" title="With the tongue" tag="Speech"
You testify that there is no god but God, and that Muhammad is His messenger.
That is the gate, and a person enters Islam through it.
:::
:::Card icon="2" title="With the heart" tag="Faith"
Certainty and ease: believing in God, His angels, His books, His messengers
and the Last Day. The heart is where faith lives — not an image, not a habit.
:::
:::Card icon="3" title="With the limbs" tag="Action"
Prayer, alms, fasting and pilgrimage; truthfulness, trustworthiness and
kindness to parents. Action brings to life what the heart has taken in, and
shows it to people.
:::
:::

:::

:::Hadith text="الدِّينُ النَّصِيحَةُ" ref="Reported by Muslim" tr="Islam is sincere advice." note="Explained as: to God, to His book, to His messenger, to the leaders of the Muslims and to the people as a whole — the root of the religion is wanting good for yourself and others."
:::

:::Section title="What does Islam offer a person?"
Why do people enter a religion they did not know? Because what they find
after knowing it exceeds what they imagined:

:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="🧭" title="A clear purpose" tag="Meaning"
You are not on earth without a role: created to worship and to be a steward,
with something to do every single day.
:::
:::Card icon="🫁" title="Tranquility" tag="Heart"
“Verily, in the remembrance of God do hearts find rest.” Anxiety settles when
the heart knows who runs things.
:::
:::Card icon="⚖️" title="A system of life" tag="Dealings"
Clear rules for prayer, family, money and justice: you know the right, and
you know the wrong.
:::
:::Card icon="🤝" title="Practical ethics" tag="Conduct"
Truth, trust, justice and mercy — not theories, but character with a reward
and a scale.
:::
:::Card icon="👥" title="One community" tag="Society"
One prayer, one mosque, one feast: you become a brother or sister to every
Muslim on earth.
:::
:::Card icon="🌅" title="Life after death" tag="Hereafter"
The Last Day is not the end of the story: what you do today has an effect
that remains.
:::
:::

:::

:::Section title="Five words you will meet often"
Do not worry about the terms — their meanings are simple, and each one has a
page on this site.

:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="🕋" title="Tawhid" tag="The core"
Devoting worship to God alone: we call on none but Him and seek help from
none but Him.
:::
:::Card icon="🕌" title="Salah" tag="Daily"
Five times a day you stand before God: the pillar of the religion and the
first thing a servant is asked about.
:::
:::Card icon="📖" title="Qur'an" tag="Revelation"
The speech of God revealed to Muhammad ﷺ; reciting it is worship and
reflecting on it is guidance.
:::
:::Card icon="☀️" title="Sunnah" tag="Guidance"
What he said, did and approved ﷺ: it explains the Qur'an and clarifies it.
:::
:::Card icon="🌙" title="Iman" tag="Heart"
Belief in God, His messengers and the Last Day; through it deeds are set
right and accepted.
:::
:::Card icon="🧾" title="Fiqh" tag="Learning"
Questions gathered in the glossary: nothing to memorise now, just to revisit
when you need it.
:::
:::

:::Section title="Questions at the beginning of the road"
:::FAQ
:::QA q="Is Islam a difficult religion?"
Its five pillars and few clear principles; the difficulty comes from the
habits people add and label as burdens. Start with the core, then build up —
this site is ordered exactly for that.
:::
:::QA q="Do I need someone to explain it to me?"
These words are written for you, and the glossary explains every term in one
line. If you find someone to discuss it with, so much the better — Islam
loves questions and loving debate.
:::
:::QA q="Can I understand it in my own language?"
Yes. The meanings of the Qur'an are translated into every language of the
world, and this site has two complete editions, Arabic and English, each page
linked to its twin.
:::
