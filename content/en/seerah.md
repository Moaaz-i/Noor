The seerah is not a chronicle of events only; it is the practical evidence of the message: how a persecuted minority becomes a community, and how a nation is built by wisdom rather than force.

:::Timeline
:::Event when="570 CE" title="The Year of the Elephant — his birth"
Muhammad ﷺ was born in Mecca; he was sent at forty and sealed the line of prophets.
:::
:::Event when="610 CE" title="Revelation begins"
The first revelation in the cave of Hira: “Read in the name of your Lord who created,” followed by three years of secret preaching.
:::
:::Event when="615 CE" title="Open preaching and persecution"
After the deaths of Abu Talib and Hamza, Quraysh resolved to kill him; he went to Taif, then the second pledge at Aqaba.
:::
:::Event when="622 CE" title="The migration to Medina"
The first Islamic polity is founded on a document: “Indeed, you are a pledge upon Islam” — community before army, order before conquest.
:::
:::Event when="624–628 CE" title="Badr, Uhud, the trench and the truce"
One lesson across four trials: victory at Badr, trial at Uhud, patience at the trench, wisdom at Hudaybiya.
:::
:::Event when="628 CE" title="The pilgrimage of restitution (ʿUmrat al-Qaḍaʾ)"
A great opening without a battle: returning with a just covenant after twelve hard years.
:::
:::Event when="630 CE" title="The opening of Mecca"
A majestic entry: “Go, for you are untied” — pardon after centuries of wrong.
:::
:::Event when="632 CE" title="Farewell and passing"
The farewell sermon in the farewell pilgrimage, and his death in Medina after the message was complete.
:::
:::

:::Section title="Three lessons from the seerah"
:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="🛡️" title="Patience as a strategy"
Patience is not an emotion but a plan: sent at forty, migrated after ten, victorious after twelve.
:::
:::Card icon="🤝" title="Covenant before authority"
Medina began with a document between its groups — the state grew out of an agreement, not a takeover.
:::
:::Card icon="🫶" title="Mercy as an end"
The Prophet ﷺ was described as the Qur’an made flesh: belief and character were one thing for him.
:::
:::

:::Section title='Key takeaways'
:::Cards cols='3'
:::Card icon='📖' title='Quiet first, public later' tag='1'
Three years of secret preaching came before anything open — depth is built in the quiet, and only then does it show.
:::
:::Card icon='🧭' title='Change starts with a move' tag='2'
The emigration cost them their homes and their wealth; one brave step can redirect a whole road faster than years of waiting.
:::
:::Card icon='🕊️' title='Pardon when you hold power' tag='3'
Mecca was entered without a battle: strength is measured by what you do when you are the one who can choose.
:::

:::Section title='Quick questions'
:::FAQ
:::QA q='Why study the seerah when we have the Qur’an?'
The Qur’an gives the guidance; the seerah shows it being lived — how people carried revelation into their homes and their dealings. One is the instruction, the other the worked example.
:::
:::QA q='Where should I start reading the seerah?'
Begin with a reliable condensed account that covers the major stations, then widen into a single station as your knowledge grows. Summary first, detail after.
:::
:::QA q='Is everything published about the seerah reliable?'
No. Reports are tested with the tools of hadith scholarship: what is sound is taken as guidance, and what is weak is mentioned with caution and never built upon.
:::
