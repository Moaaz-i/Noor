:::FAQ
:::QA q="Is Islam a religion of violence?"
No. The Qur’an prescribes wisdom and gentleness in invitation: “Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction,” and forbids killing a soul without right. The rules often labelled “war” are in truth rules of constraint: forbidding the killing of women, the elderly and children, the cutting of trees and the breaking of covenants.
:::
:::QA q="Is freedom of choice real in Islam?"
Yes: “There is no compulsion in religion.” Invitation is by wisdom rather than force, and reward and punishment are meaningless without responsibility — a settled principle of the creed.
:::
:::QA q="What is Islam’s view of a woman?"
Her dignity is an original given: her wealth, her education, her reputation and her right to choose are affirmed. Rights of kinship, inheritance and upkeep are set in the texts themselves.
:::
:::QA q="Is Islam against science and technology?"
Knowledge is a duty on every Muslim, and inquiry, experiment and inference are parts of the legal method itself. Apparent tension comes from misreading, not from a real conflict.
:::
:::QA q="Why five prayers a day?"
A daily rhythm for the soul: two rak‘ahs at dawn to start, two at noon to break fatigue, two at sunset to stand at account, two at night to rest, and two before dawn to prepare.
:::
:::QA q="Is zakat a tax?"
A similar administration, a different essence: zakat is an act of worship with fixed recipients, set by sacred law rather than changed by authority, and it cannot be waived by ability alone.
:::
:::QA q="I am new to all this — where do I start?"
Three steps: read the [pillars](/en/pillars), then [beliefs](/en/beliefs), then take the [quiz](/en/quiz) to see what is still missing.
:::
:::QA q="Do I need Arabic to understand the Qur’an?"
A translation opens the door and gives the general meaning. Understanding vocabulary and structure, though, needs some Arabic — which is what the [glossary](/en/glossary) here supports.
:::
:::QA q="What is the difference between Islam, faith and excellence (ihsan)?"
Islam is what appears in action, faith is what the heart affirms, and ihsan is to worship God as if you see Him. The three depend on one another.
:::
:::QA q="Is Islam an Arab religion?"
No: “Every child is born upon the natural disposition,” and this message seals the line of prophets and is addressed to all of humanity.
:::
