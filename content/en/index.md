## Your gateway to Islam

Here you will find structured teaching that explains Islam in clear language:
we begin with a page that answers what Islam is for someone who knows nothing,
then the five pillars, then deep belief, the Quran, the Prophetic biography,
noble character and the landmarks of Islamic history — ending at a glossary
and an interactive quiz that measures what you learned.

Every page stands alone or can be taught in a session, with references from
the Book of God ﷺ and the practice of the Prophet ﷺ, and a plain meaning for
every term you meet on the way.

### How to get the most out of it?

- **Start from zero:** read What is Islam? then Pillars and Beliefs.
- **Tie knowledge to evidence:** the Quran and Seerah pages lead with proof.
- **Review regularly:** return to the concepts cards, then take the quiz.
- **Correct misunderstandings:** check any term in the glossary as you go.

:::Section title='Questions a beginner asks' subtitle='Short answers that are enough to start'
:::QA q='What is the essence of Islam?'
Monotheism: dedicating religion to God alone and following His messenger ﷺ
in what he conveyed from his Lord.
:::
:::QA q='Is Islam a religion of belief only?'
No; it is a practical message of worship, conduct, and character — beginning
in the heart and appearing in the limbs.
:::
:::QA q='Where do I start?'
Start with the testimony of monotheism, then learn the prayer, and read the
pillars page until the full picture appears.
:::
:::QA q='How do I learn more?'
Take one page a day, review the concept cards, and test yourself at the end
of the week.
:::
