The Prophet ﷺ said: “I was sent to perfect noble character.” For Muslims character is not an optional refinement but part of worship and the primary means of invitation.

:::Hadith text="إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ لِأُتَمِّمَ مَكَارِمَ الْأَخْلَاقِ" ref="Reported by Ahmad and al-Hakim" tr="“I was sent to perfect noble character.”" note="الرسالة جمعت بين التشريع والخلق: واحدٌ لا منفصلان."
:::

:::Section title="Core character"
:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="🗣️" title="Truthfulness"
“Truthfulness leads to righteousness.” In dealings it builds trust, which shortens cost and distance.
:::
:::Card icon="🤝" title="Trustworthiness"
Guarding wealth, secrets and office. When trust is lost, stewardship is lost — this underlies every contract.
:::
:::Card icon="⚖️" title="Justice"
“O you who believe, be persistently standing firm in justice.” Justice toward enemies comes before favour toward friends.
:::
:::Card icon="👪" title="Honouring parents"
“And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and to parents, good treatment” — immediately after monotheism.
:::
:::Card icon="🌳" title="Kinship"
It lengthens life, gathers wealth and prevents poverty — paired with oneness of God in a single verse.
:::
:::Card icon="🤲" title="Shame (ḥayaʾ)"
“Shame is a branch of faith.” On it, indecency in conduct or curiosity in meddling are refused.
:::
:::Card icon="💡" title="Gentleness"
“Gentleness was not placed in anything except that it adorned it.” It does not oppose firmness; it improves its use.
:::
:::Card icon="🚪" title="Self-accountability & covering faults"
Auditing the self before the public audit, and concealing people’s failings unless there is open harm.
:::
:::Card icon="🌱" title="Repentance"
It erases what came before — a standing hope whose doors stay open while the servant lives.
:::
:::

:::
:::Section title="Ethics in the market and among neighbours"
:::Cards cols="2"
:::Card icon="🏪" title="Ethical economy"
“None of you owns anything except three: his food, his clothing and his endowment” — then the command to fulfil and hand over goods, and the blessing named in the transaction.
:::
:::Card icon="🏘️" title="Neighbours & compassion"
The right of the neighbour runs forty days, the neighbour of the Qur’an comes first, and the poor have a known share.
:::

:::Section title="How to master one trait"
:::Timeline
:::Event when="Week 1" title="Choose a single trait"
Do not start with ten; pick truthfulness or gentleness and write down one measurable behaviour.
:::
:::Event when="Week 2–3" title="Watch yourself daily"
Note the moments the old habit failed, and revisit them at night without over-analysing.
:::
:::Event when="Week 4" title="Ask the people around you"
Character shows in other people’s eyes more than in your own feelings — ask those who live with you.
:::

:::Section title='Key takeaways'
:::Cards cols='3'
:::Card icon='📜' title='Character by command' tag='1'
Justice and kindness are a duty, not a taste: the command to do good and refuse the ugly comes from God, not from custom.
:::
:::Card icon='🪞' title='Measured in deeds, not words' tag='2'
Nobody is known as truthful by claiming it; character is the repeated conduct of private and public life.
:::
:::Card icon='🪨' title='Shown under pressure' tag='3'
A person shows himself when he is angry and when he is short-changed; fair-weather manners do not count.
:::
:::
:::

:::Section title='This week’s steps'
:::Cards cols='3'
:::Card icon='📞' title='Reach a relative'
Call someone in your family you have not heard from in a while, and begin by asking how they are rather than with what you need.
:::
:::Card icon='🤫' title='Cover and pass over'
If you hear of a fault in your brother, let it go today: do not carry it on, and do not comment on it.
:::
:::Card icon='⚖️' title='Honesty in the sale'
Give the buyer the full measure you would want for yourself: complete weight, a true description, and delivery on time.
:::

:::Section title='Quick questions'
:::FAQ
:::QA q='Is character innate or learned?'
The fitra leans toward the good, but habit fixes whatever you repeat — so character is built by small, steady steps rather than by a burst of enthusiasm.
:::
:::QA q='What is the difference between gentleness and weakness?'
Gentleness is in the manner while the right is held firm; weakness is giving up the right and accepting injustice. Be gentle, and do not hand over the balance.
:::
:::QA q='Does character differ from society to society?'
Its forms change with custom, but its constants do not: justice, trustworthiness and the rights of others hold in every time and place.
:::
