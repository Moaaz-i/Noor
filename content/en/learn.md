This site is not a book to read once but a path to travel in stages. The line between those who start and those who finish is simply having a plan small enough to keep.

:::Section title="The path in four stages"
:::Cards cols="4"
:::Card icon="١" title="Foundations" kicker="Week 1"
Read the three doors: pillars, beliefs, ethics. The goal at this stage is the picture, not memorisation.
:::
:::Card icon="٢" title="Depth" kicker="Week 2–3"
Then the Qur’an and Sunnah, then seerah and history. Add one timeline and record five terms.
:::
:::Card icon="٣" title="Practice" kicker="Week 4"
Turn three ideas into habits: one fast day, one truthfulness practice, one act of kinship.
:::
:::Card icon="٤" title="Teach" kicker="Week 5"
Explain what you learned to another person in your own language. Explanation is the real test of understanding.
:::
:::

:::
:::Section title="A suggested week"
| Day | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Read one new door | 25 min |
| Sunday | Review + 5 terms | 15 min |
| Monday | Listen to a lecture | 30 min |
| Tuesday | Take the quiz here | 10 min |
| Wednesday | Read a primary text (hadith / ayah) | 20 min |
| Thursday | Write a five-line summary | 10 min |
| Friday | Practical application + weekly review | 15 min |

:::
:::Section title="Forms this site supports"
:::Cards cols="3"
:::Card icon="📖" title="Written text"
Focused pages: definition, effect, one practical step — no padding.
:::
:::Card icon="⏳" title="Timeline"
For seerah and history: the event in one line, the lesson in the next.
:::
:::Card icon="🗂️" title="Comparison table"
When two ideas blur together, a table separates them in a second.
:::
:::Card icon="🎮" title="Self-test"
Six questions with instant feedback and an explanation after each answer.
:::
:::Card icon="🗜️" title="Glossary"
Twenty-four core terms with Latin transliteration and instant filtering.
:::
:::Card icon="🗣️" title="Teaching others"
The hardest and strongest form: if you can explain it, you have understood it.
:::
:::

:::
:::Section title="Suggested starting sources"
:::Cards cols="2"
:::Card icon="📚" title="Qur’an & tafsir"
Begin with a simple English translation of the meaning, then a modern tafsir, then al-Tabari for comprehensiveness.
:::
:::Card icon="🎧" title="Seerah & hadith"
Ibn Hisham for the biography; the Muwatta of Malik and the two sahihs for the science of narration.
:::
